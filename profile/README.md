# BIGBOARD.GAMES

**Play on one big board.** Push two to four phones or tablets together, edge to edge, and
they become one game board: the puck leaves your screen and slides onto your friend's.
[Play now at bigboard.games](https://bigboard.games/play), free in the browser.

- **No app, no sign-up.** Open [bigboard.games](https://bigboard.games) on each device and you're at the same table.
- **Any mix of devices.** iPhone and Android, phones and tablets, side by side.
- **Devices find each other.** On the same Wi-Fi they join by themselves; otherwise scan a QR code or type a four-letter code.
- **Party games for 1–4 devices.** Play head to head, in teams, or with up to four players around one tablet.

## Games

| Game | What it is |
| --- | --- |
| [Seam Hockey](https://bigboard.games/games/seam-hockey) | Air hockey across 2–4 phones and tablets. Hit the puck and watch it slide off your screen onto your friend's. |
| [Hot Potato](https://bigboard.games/games/hot-potato) | Flick the hot potato onto someone else's screen before your fuse burns out. 2–4 devices, or one shared screen. |
| [Whack-a-Mole](https://bigboard.games/games/whack-a-mole) | Bop the moles on every screen before they hide: head to head, as a team, or alone against the clock. |
| [Spillover](https://bigboard.games/games/spillover) | Two devices top edge to top edge: draw a shape and drop it on the other side. Don't let your pile spill over. |
| [Islands](https://bigboard.games/games/islands) | Two devices become one sheet of grid paper. Ring the other player's dots to capture an island. |

**Coming soon:** [Tilt Run](https://bigboard.games/games/tilt-run) (roll a marble through a maze across the screens),
[Shoal](https://bigboard.games/games/shoal) (herd a glowing shoal into the net together),
[Lighthouse](https://bigboard.games/games/lighthouse) (bounce one beam across every screen) and
[Pinball](https://bigboard.games/games/pinball) (a new table every game).
