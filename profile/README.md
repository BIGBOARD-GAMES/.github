# BIGBOARD.GAMES

BIGBOARD.GAMES is a collection of free browser games for the same room: phones or tablets
laid edge to edge become one board, or one device is passed around. An air hockey puck, a hot
potato or a chess piece leaves one screen and comes in on the next.

**Play at [bigboard.games](https://bigboard.games).**

- **Free, no app, no sign-up.** Open bigboard.games in the browser on each device.
- **Any mix of devices.** iPhone and Android, phones and tablets, side by side.
- **Devices find each other.** One device starts a table; on the same Wi-Fi the others find
  it by themselves, or join with its QR code or four-letter code.
- **Not only side by side.** Friends far away can join by link, most games also play on one
  shared screen, and bots can fill empty seats.
- **15 games in 8 languages:** English, Ukrainian, Spanish, Russian, German, Polish,
  Chinese (Simplified) and Portuguese (Brazil).

## Games

| Game | Players | What it is |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Air Hockey](https://bigboard.games/games/air-hockey) | 2–12 | Push 2–4 phones or tablets together and the puck slides from one screen onto the next. Duels or teams; two can share one phone. |
| [Hot Potato](https://bigboard.games/games/hot-potato) | 2–12 | One potato slides across every screen. Flick it onto someone else's before your fuse burns out. |
| [Tower Defense](https://bigboard.games/games/tower-defense) | 1–4 | Co-op: one road winds across every screen to the lantern the whole table defends. |
| [Billiards](https://bigboard.games/games/pool) | 2–8 | 8-Ball, 9-Ball, snooker, Killer and Cutthroat, on one table across the screens or on one phone passed between shots. |
| [Paddle Tennis](https://bigboard.games/games/paddle-tennis) | 2–12 | The ball you return flies off your screen onto your friend's. Duels, teams or free-for-all. |
| [Snake](https://bigboard.games/games/snake) | 1–12 | The devices make one field and every player steers their own snake. The last snake in wins the round. |
| [Mole Pop](https://bigboard.games/games/mole-pop) | 1–12 | Whack-a-mole on every screen at the table: head to head, as one team, or alone against the clock. |
| [Tilt Run](https://bigboard.games/games/tilt-run) | 1–12 | Tilt your device to roll one marble through a maze that runs across every screen. |
| [Islands](https://bigboard.games/games/islands) | 2 | A dots game for two: place dots in turn and surround the other player's dots to capture them. |
| [Chess](https://bigboard.games/games/chess) | 2–3 | Two phones laid top edge to top edge make one board; three players play on a hexagon. |
| [Checkers](https://bigboard.games/games/checkers) | 2–4 | Russian or English rules, for two, three or four players. |
| [Go](https://bigboard.games/games/go) | 2–4 | Surround stones to capture them and wall off the most board. Pair Go at four. |
| [Sea Battle](https://bigboard.games/games/sea-battle) | 2–6 | Hide your fleet on your own phone, then fire at theirs, in turns or in volleys. |
| [Secret Words](https://bigboard.games/games/secret-words) | 4–12 | A team word game: each team's Guide sees the secret map and gives one-word hints. One phone each. |
| [Spillover](https://bigboard.games/games/spillover) | 2 | Draw a shape on your grid and it drops onto your friend's side. Overfill their well before they fill yours. |

All games: [bigboard.games/games](https://bigboard.games/games).
